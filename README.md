### 🌍 Building for Africa

> *Africa's connectivity constraints are not problems to route around — they are the design brief.*

- **Offline-first** — edge AI over cloud dependency
- **CPU-only** — no expensive GPUs required
- **Mobile-first** — where most Africans access the internet
- **Data sovereign** — processing stays on-device and on-continent
